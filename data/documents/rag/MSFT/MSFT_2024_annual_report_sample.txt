BUSINESS OVERVIEW
Microsoft develops software, cloud services, devices, and gaming products.

ITEM 1A RISK FACTORS
Microsoft faces intense competition across cloud services, software, gaming, search, and artificial intelligence. Cybersecurity incidents, service outages, and failures to protect customer data could harm customer trust and operating results. The company also depends on components, skilled employees, and global supply chains.

OUTLOOK
Management monitors cloud demand, customer technology spending, foreign exchange, regulation, and technological change.
